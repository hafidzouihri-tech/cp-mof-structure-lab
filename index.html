What is computed

Bond graph. Each CIF block is expanded with its symmetry operators (minor disorder components and low-occupancy atoms optionally removed). A periodic cell-list search builds covalent bonds between non-metals (d < r₁ + r₂ + 0.30 Å; H attached to its nearest atom) and metal–donor bonds to O, N, S, P, halogens (and C if “M–C” is ticked). Each metal has an M–O cut-off (editable); cut-offs for other donors are shifted by the difference of covalent radii.

Dimensionality. Every connected component is walked with integer cell offsets; reaching an atom again with a different offset reveals a translation, and the rank of the translation lattice gives 0D (discrete complex), 1D (chain, direction [uvw]), 2D (layer, plane (hkl)) or 3D (framework). For 3D frameworks, interpenetration = number of separate nets (independent components × sub-lattice index). Components without metal are guests or counter-ions.

SBUs and topology. Metals sharing a donor atom (μ-O, μ-Cl, μ-S…) or linked by a three-atom bridge (O–C–O, N–N, N–C–N) with M···M ≤ the set limit form one SBU (finite cluster or infinite rod). Ligands binding two or more SBUs are linkers. The number of linkers around a finite SBU is its connectivity (n-c node), which is the input for a topological analysis in ToposPro or Systre.

Ligands. For each ligand:

  • formula and tentative name (bdc, btc, 4,4′-bpy, pyz, im, mim, phen, ox, OAc, formate, tu, DMF …);
  • denticity κ and number of metals bound μ;
  • chelate ring sizes;
  • carboxylate binding modes in Harris notation x.y₁y₂, with syn/anti for μ₂ bridges;
  • linker role and aromatic rings.

Metal centres. CN and donor set, plus:

  • bond lengths: ⟨d⟩, DI(d);
  • angles: σ², ⟨λ⟩, τ₄/τ₅;
  • shape: ECoN and continuous shape measures for CN 2–9 (exact for CN ≤ 4, Hungarian/quaternion search for CN ≥ 5), with shape maps and minimal-distortion paths;
  • bond-valence sums with R₀ for M–O, M–N, M–S, M–Cl, M–Br, M–I.

The CN 7–9 reference polyhedra are idealised geometries, so their S values are approximate. Report S values from SHAPE if exact numbers are needed.

Interactions. N/O–H···A hydrogen bonds; atoms closer than three bonds (e.g. polyhedron edges) are excluded. π–π stacking between planar 5/6-membered C/N rings: Cg···Cg, interplanar angle α, offset angle β, perpendicular distance and slippage.

Porosity. A grid map with van der Waals radii gives:

  • empty volume;
  • probe-accessible volume (union of probe spheres, PLATON-like);
  • largest included sphere (LCD);
  • channel dimensionality from percolation.

Free guests and counter-ions can be removed first (activated framework). Crystal density is computed from the CIF content.

Figures. All charts and 3D views are SVG with explicit colours; the PNG export writes the chosen dpi into the file. Series are coloured by dimensionality (0D blue circle, 1D orange square, 2D green triangle, 3D purple diamond), with a palette checked for colour-vision deficiency.
